  • Residential & commercial cleaning

    Routine and deep cleaning for homes, offices, and commercial spaces.

  • Post-construction & move-in/move-out cleaning

    Full site cleanup after renovation or construction, and turnover cleaning between tenants.

  • Sanitation & disinfection services

We also provide:

  • Debt collection

    Structured, compliant collection work that keeps accuracy and professionalism at the center of every recovery.

  • Conflict resolution

    Neutral mediation for workplace and client disputes, including oversight of the confidential reporting channel below.

02

What this channel is for

Debt collection work and conflict resolution both depend on trust and trust breaks when people have no safe way to flag a problem. Klean Hands exists so that anyone touched by that work has somewhere to put a concern down without it becoming a spectacle.

  • Workplace fraud or falsified records

    Misrepresented debts, altered account histories, improper fee assessment, or falsified collection notes.

  • Misconduct

    Harassment, coercive collection tactics, abuse of authority, or conduct that violates professional standards.

  • Threats

    Intimidation directed at staff, clients, or third parties verbal, written, or implied.

  • Unresolved disputes

    A disagreement that standard channels haven't been able to settle, and needs a neutral party to look at it.

03

How a report is handled

  1. You submit the report

    Fill in the intake form below. Include what happened, when, and who was involved, in as much or as little detail as you're comfortable with.

  2. It reaches one person

    Reports go directly to the conflict resolution officer's private inbox, not a shared queue, not a ticketing system.

  3. It gets reviewed within 3 business days

    Every report is read and assessed. If you left contact details, you'll hear back with next steps or questions.

  4. Resolution stays proportionate and private

    Action is taken discreetly wherever possible, no public escalation unless you ask for it or safety requires it.

05

How confidentiality is protected

  • One recipient

    Reports are delivered directly to the conflict resolution officer. There is no intermediate system, dashboard, or shared team inbox.

  • No server-side storage

    This form doesn't save submissions to a database. It sends once, directly, and nothing persists on this site.

  • Contact details stay private

    Your name and email are used only to review your report and follow up, never shared beyond that, and never published anywhere.

  • No retaliation

    Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation against the reporter.

06

Who reviews these reports

Reports are reviewed personally, not routed through a compliance department. The goal is a fair, private resolution, not publicity, and not punishment for coming forward.
